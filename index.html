            Measurement notes
            

              One warm-up, then three monitored execute-and-fetch runs per variant. Median timing
              covers at most 101 rows fetched; only 100 are displayed. VM work is an estimate in
              1,000-instruction increments. Setup and index construction are excluded from query
              timing. Progress monitoring adds overhead, and timing varies by host. These are
              illustrative local measurements.
